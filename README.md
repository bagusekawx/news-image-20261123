# The World Today — Monday, 23 November 2026

A one-page newspaper front page summing up the world news of Monday, 23 November 2026. Lead story: the COP31 climate summit in Antalya collapses with no deal after nearly 200 nations fail to agree on fossil-fuel language. Also covered: Ukrainian sea drones sink the Russian corvette Merkury off Libya; 24 dead and dozens missing in a candy factory fire in Cikande, Banten, Indonesia; and Democrats winning at least 258 US House seats, their largest majority since 1992. Facts verified against Reuters, CNN and Associated Press reporting. The front page is rendered by `gen.py` (Pillow) into `entry.png`; `main.py` copies it to the harness output directory.
